# VentureLens AI — MVP Design Scope

> **Hosted scope amendment — 2026-10-21:** Retain the nine business screen templates below and add login, password change/session recovery, and administrator account management for **multiple evaluators sharing one workspace**. [Technical specification](technical-spec.md) §§5, 7, and 13 govern permissions, concurrent edits, and the added account surfaces. Earlier local-only/no-login assumptions are superseded.

**Updated:** 2026-10-18 · **Reference:** [product-spec.md](product-spec.md), v1.4  
**Scope:** 9 screen templates, shared panels/dialogs, 1 printable report layout. Persian RTL, light mode, desktop and mobile. This is the concise design inventory; the product specification governs business rules and proposed defaults.

**Visual reference — adopted 2026-10-22:** [design.md](../design.md) is the user-supplied visual design reference, preserved unchanged from `mci.ir-DESIGN.md`. Use its blue/orange palette, light surfaces, rectilinear geometry, spacing, typography direction, and restrained elevation for future VentureLens UI designs. It supersedes the earlier visual palette/spacing defaults, while this document continues to define screens and interactions. The MCI name, telecom content, and imagery describe the source reference; VentureLens retains its own identity and product content.

Apply the light theme only. Prefer DPSans/DPSansEn when font files with suitable redistribution rights are available; use locally packaged Vazirmatn as the implementation fallback. Treat measured `0px`/`1px` text, missing mobile navigation, and extreme stacking values as extraction observations to adapt, not requirements to reproduce. Keep text readable, navigation usable, and controls accessible; validate contrast and touch areas. Add the missing semantic statuses and loading/error/disabled states needed by the product. A derived dark palette in the reference does not add dark mode to scope.

S3–S8 are tabs within one assessment. S6 is one reusable template for all six domains. S7 has Risks and Gaps views, not separate full pages. Processing and failures appear within these screens, not in an additional wizard.

**Shared header:** startup, assessment ID/date/stage, lifecycle, Review status, Provisional evidence label when applicable, predecessor link, assessment tabs. Link to assessment context for rubric and jurisdiction details. Historical views always identify the selected version. Running analysis shows progress; failed/cancelled analysis shows Analysis Incomplete separately from evidence limitations. A new draft is not labeled a failed or incomplete run.

## 2. Inputs

**Startup dialog:** name required; industry, website, city, funding stage, business model and description optional. Funding stages: Pre-Seed, Seed, Series A, Series B, Growth. Business-model choices: B2B, B2C, B2B2C, B2G only.

**Assessment context on S4:** prefill stage from the startup profile, or retain the predecessor's stage for a copy, and show its origin. Allow Confirm / Change / Unknown; keep product maturity separate. Show the approved rubric version and assessment jurisdiction, with a contextual Settings link and optional entity/contract exceptions under Legal. Missing or unsupported context explains affected limitations without blocking unrelated analysis. Freeze context when queued; later profile/Settings changes do not rewrite locked assessments. See specification Sections 5.7 and 9.4.

The compact manual fields below remain the proposed field subset from specification Section 6.1; they do not add the deferred comprehensive questionnaires.

| Domain              | Fields                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Commercial          | Customer/problem; monetization/pricing; traction/period; competitors; acquisition approach.                                                                                           |
| Product             | Use case; maturity; capabilities; adoption/retention evidence; limitations; roadmap.                                                                                                  |
| Technology          | Architecture/stack; dependencies; security/reliability evidence; incidents/constraints; engineering ownership.                                                                        |
| Financial           | Revenue; cash/as-of date; expenses/burn/period; liabilities; funding need/use.                                                                                                        |
| Team & Organization | Founders/roles/commitment; experience; headcount/vacancies; execution evidence; key-person dependencies.                                                                              |
| Legal               | Entity/registration; ownership; IP claims; material obligations/disputes; supporting documents; optional jurisdiction exception with entity/contract scope and source or attribution. |

Each section includes context, evidence references, answer status, and Save Draft/Save & Continue/Complete Later. Metric inputs show applicable unit/currency/period. Missing answers never block saving; invalid draft text is preserved and excluded from calculations.

**Content selection on S4:** use the file list/source viewer to include or exclude files and supported extracted units such as pages, slides, or worksheets. Label included hidden content. Distinguish Excluded by you from Unreadable / not extracted; if a smaller unit cannot be isolated reliably, offer exclusion of its containing worksheet/file. Show selected scope and resulting gaps, retain selection across navigation/copying, and freeze it when queued. Excluded content and derived summaries are outside analysis and transmission; never send the full original as a fallback. Explain that exclusion leaves the local original stored. This is selection, not an arbitrary redaction editor. See specification Section 7.2.

The readiness panel is not a promise that domains will receive scores. Unknown readiness displays Needs review; no hidden paid analysis runs merely to populate the panel.

Keep context and content details expandable within existing screens. Start uses one concise confirmation; unchanged retries reuse sharing authorization. Selected files still being read must finish, or be explicitly excluded, before start. After lock, selection/context changes use the same corrected-assessment action as other factual changes.

## 4. User Journeys

| Journey                        | Path                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First use                      | Workspace readiness notice → Settings if setup is needed → return → create startup. Local preparation remains available without a working provider.                                                                                                                                                           |
| New assessment                 | Documents & Inputs → upload/manual entry → review compact assessment context, content scope and critical values → readiness/start panel → analysis → Overview → domain/evidence/risk review.                                                                                                                  |
| Select shared content          | File/source review → inspect included hidden content and adjust supported selection if needed → see exclusions/gaps → review transmission summary in the start panel. No extra sharing wizard or per-page confirmation.                                                                                       |
| Missing assessment context     | Stage Unknown or missing/unsupported jurisdiction → explain affected conclusions → enter context or continue with limitations. Settings returns to the same draft; context changes after lock use a successor.                                                                                                |
| Sparse evidence                | Readiness shows known gaps → Analyze available information → supported findings and explicit Not Assessable reasons → provisional opinion or Insufficient Evidence → review/export.                                                                                                                           |
| Conflicting evidence           | Conflict summary → affected alternatives → resolve from existing evidence or Keep unresolved → review scenario-dependent opinion.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Approval/export                | Readiness checklist → follow links to fix actual blockers → acknowledge limitations → approve → export with approval and provisional labels intact. Draft export remains available.                                                                                                                           |
| Correct facts/add evidence     | Locked field/gap → Create corrected assessment → copy preview → new draft opens relevant field → edit → explicitly start fresh analysis → new review/approval.                                                                                                                                                |
| Change reviewed interpretation | Edit finding/opinion → save-impact notice if approved → new review revision → inspect changes → update opinion → approve again. Cancelled edits preserve approval.                                                                                                                                            |
| Recover failure                | Error names failed task, saved work and recovery → retry/resume unchanged stage, or export Draft/Incomplete when permitted → successful recovery restores technical readiness → review/approve. Provider fixes return to the same context; factual fixes use a successor. Disclose possible additional usage. |
| Remove/purge                   | Draft removal affects that draft only. Global purge → preview all affected records → confirm → actual completion or remaining-scope/retry message.                                                                                                                                                            |

## 5. Required States and Rules

| State                                         | Required presentation / next action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Empty                                         | Explain what belongs here and offer the relevant create/upload/enter action.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Saving / save failed                          | Show actual save state; preserve text; retry. Do not lock an unsaved snapshot.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Reading / partly readable / unreadable        | Distinguish uploaded from usable; show omissions; replace file or enter information manually without upgrading evidence quality.                                                                                                                                      |
| Missing provider / temporary failure          | Explain setup versus retry; link to Settings with return path; retain local work.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Running / cancelled / partially failed        | Honest stage progress; saved results; cancel/retry options; cancellation does not unlock inputs.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Not analyzed / In progress                    | No validated result yet; show progress or a recovery action. Do not substitute zero or Not Assessable for unfinished processing.                                                                                                                                      |
| Not Assessable                                | Exact unmet requirements, supported findings and priority evidence requests; Add evidence routes to draft inputs or a successor.                                                                                                                                      |
| Not Applicable                                | Show the justified applicability reason; no score where all scored criteria are inapplicable, and no coverage percentage with an empty denominator. Missing evidence is not a justification. Keep all six domains visible.                                            |
| Applicability needs review                    | Explain unresolved context, mark coverage provisional and link to input/review; withhold affected scores until eligibility is established. Unaffected results remain usable.                                                                                          |
| Scenario-dependent                            | Overview summary and Compare alternatives; labeled per-scenario scores or eligibility states in details and export. No default winner or averaged score. Keep material conflicts visible even when scores match.                                                      |
| Unresolved conflict                           | No default winning source; compare affected scenarios; keep unresolved or resolve with rationale.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Analysis Incomplete / Draft/Incomplete report | Identify unfinished/failed stages and affected sections; retain validated results; link to retry/resume and offer permitted draft export. Explain that approval requires technical completion. This state is distinct from a completed analysis with sparse evidence. |
| Outdated opinion / approval blocked           | Visible checklist with direct actions; update opinion/review/save. Missing domain scores alone do not block approval.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Approved and Provisional                      | Separate labels: human approval does not establish sufficient evidence. Show the same labels before and after export.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Historical / archived                         | Identify selected assessment/report version; open current record or restore archive; never silently rewrite historical data.                                                                                                                                          |

- No unexplained disabled primary action or dead-end error screen.
- A successfully queued run locks its inputs; a failed start request does not. New facts after lock require a successor; old inputs/reports remain intact except explicit purge.
- Opening an editor or source does not invalidate approval. Saving substantive review changes does; previous approved reports remain available.
- Keep source navigation, Settings return paths and correction links contextual; preserve position and entered work.
- Disclose possible paid processing before starting, retrying, or requesting a new opinion synthesis. Do not promise free reruns or invented estimates.
- Show consequence warnings at the relevant transition, not repeatedly during routine navigation.
- Use one primary result with relevant qualifiers and a short reason; keep calculation details expandable. Apply specification Section 10.4 consistently across Overview, Domains and reports. These result presentations do not add lifecycle states.
- Keep all four measures distinct: Questions Answered, evidence coverage, performance score, and confidence. Provisional coverage does not replace the separate opinion/evidence-limitation label.

## 6. Printable Report and Design Boundary

**One report layout:** cover/status → available opinion → scope/limitations → six-domain overview/details → risks → conflicts/scenarios → gaps/questions/actions → citations and approval attribution. Include Draft/Approved, Incomplete when processing is unfinished, Provisional when applicable, assessment/revision identifiers, stage/jurisdiction/rubric context, and readable Persian print formatting. Summarize input exclusions without reproducing excluded content. Use the same applicability and scenario distinctions as the screens. See specification Sections 10.4 and 15.

**Incomplete report variant, same layout:** under the proposed export policy in specification Section 12.3, show Draft/Incomplete on the cover and every page; list unfinished/failed stages and affected sections near the summary. Retain validated results, mark absent outcomes Not analyzed, and use Opinion unavailable — analysis incomplete if synthesis has not succeeded. In-app approval links to recovery and stays blocked until technical completion; the exported explanation stands on its own. Evidence gaps alone do not make a completed assessment technically incomplete. Preview all labels before export and preserve historical report versions after recovery.

**Design at 375px and desktop, verify at 768/1280/1440px.** Include keyboard focus, readable contrast, non-color status labels, and contained scrolling for wide evidence tables.

**Excluded:** login/multiuser screens, founder portal, dark mode, advanced dashboards, portfolio comparison, automated assessment-diff tools, and extensive questionnaires. No additional full-page readiness, analysis-progress, or approval wizard is needed.

**Review checks:** Walk through sparse evidence, a misread financial value, an unresolved conflict, a failed run, a changed approved opinion, a shared-source purge, content exclusions/hidden sheets, unknown or changed assessment context, applicability states, and incomplete export/recovery. Every case must expose the consequence before commitment and provide a clear next action without adding a mandatory page or losing entered work. Use acceptance scenarios A23–A34 in the product specification and the Persian terminology in Section 17.4.
